    About the Almanac authority · craft · wild fermentation

    The Pepperwood Bread Almanac is a living journal dedicated to the art, science, and culture of bread. Founded in 2026, we document the baker’s craft from grain to crumb — with rigor, curiosity, and a deep respect for tradition.

    ⟡ our mission

    To cultivate a deeper understanding of bread through rigorous testing, clear writing, and a shared passion for fermentation. We bridge the gap between home baker and artisan, between tradition and innovation.

    — The Pepperwood Collective

    ⟡ what we believe

    Bread is a living document. Every loaf tells a story of time, temperature, and touch. We believe in slow knowledge, honest technique, and the beauty of a well‑scored boule.

    — from the editorial charter
