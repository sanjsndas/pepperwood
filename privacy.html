    🔏 privacy data · respect · transparency

    We protect your dough and your data.
    — read our pledge

    🕊️ our commitment

    At Pepperwood Almanac, we believe that privacy is a fundamental ingredient in a healthy relationship with our readers. This policy outlines the types of personal information we receive and collect when you visit our site, as well as how we safeguard your information. We never sell or share your data with third parties, except as required by law.

    📋 information we collect

    We collect minimal information to improve your experience and to respond to inquiries. This includes:

    • Contact details — name, email address, and message content when you fill out our contact form.
    • Usage data — anonymized analytics to understand which articles are most read, helping us bake better content.
    • Cookies — small text files that remember your preferences (you can disable them at any time).

    🍪 cookies & tracking

    We use essential cookies to ensure the website functions properly. We also use optional analytics cookies to track aggregate visitor behavior. You can manage your cookie preferences through your browser settings. For more details, see our Cookies Policy.

    🔐 how we protect your data

    We implement industry-standard security measures, including SSL encryption, firewalls, and regular security audits. Access to personal data is restricted to authorized editors and technical staff who are bound by confidentiality agreements.

    📬 your rights

    You have the right to:

    • Request a copy of the data we hold about you.
    • Ask us to correct or delete your personal information.
    • Withdraw consent for marketing communications at any time.

    To exercise these rights, please contact us at editor@pepperwood.com.

    📅 updates to this policy

    We may update this policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. We will notify you of any material changes by posting the new policy on this page with a revised date.
