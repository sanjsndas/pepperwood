    🍪 cookies crumbs · consent · control

    We use cookies to bake a better experience.
    — your choice matters

    📌 what are cookies?

    Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device when you visit a website. They help us remember your preferences, understand how you interact with our content, and improve your overall experience. At Pepperwood Almanac, we use cookies responsibly — like a master baker uses a starter.

    🍪 types of cookies we use

    We categorise cookies based on their purpose and lifespan. Here’s a clear breakdown:

    CategoryPurposeDuration
    EssentialEnable core functionality (e.g., navigation, form submissions)Session / persistent
    PreferencesRemember your settings (e.g., dark mode, language)1 year
    AnalyticsCollect anonymised data on page visits and interactionsup to 2 years
    MarketingWe do not use marketing or tracking cookies from third parties.—

    🛠️ your control & consent

    You are in charge. You can manage or delete cookies at any time through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to block or remove cookies, but please note that disabling essential cookies may affect the performance of the site.

    • Browser controls: Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge — all offer cookie management tools.
    • Opt-out: You can disable analytics cookies by adjusting your browser settings or using our cookie preference tool (coming soon).
    • Withdraw consent: You can withdraw your consent at any time by clearing your cookies.

    🔐 third-party cookies

    We do not share your data with third-party advertisers. However, we may embed content (e.g., videos, maps) that use their own cookies. These are governed by the respective third-party policies. We recommend reviewing their privacy policies before engaging with embedded content.

    📅 updates & changes

    This policy may be updated to reflect changes in technology or legal requirements. We will always post the revised date at the bottom of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
